# Requirements

## Programme Information and Updates

- About the Program page with history, structure, photos, videos, testimonials, and past winners.
- Live announcements and updates section.

## Student Resources and Learning Enablement

- Resource library with prep material, recorded masterclasses, and webinars.
- Content categorized by competition stage.
- Webinar scheduling, live streaming integration, and recording archive.

## Student Registration and Data Validation

- Registration form matching the existing Zoho form.
- Mandatory disclaimers.
- Email and mobile OTP verification.
- Duplicate prevention by email and mobile.
- Automated confirmation emails after successful registration.

## Integrated Communication System

- Admin bulk email and WhatsApp communication for 10,000+ students.
- Domain-based email recommended for deliverability.
- Communication logs for tracking sent messages.

## Video Pitch and Submission Integration

- Team uploads for video solution and PDF deck.
- Time-bound submission windows.
- Automatic rejection of late submissions.
- Email confirmation for accepted or rejected entries.

## Regional Round 1 Scheduling and Hosting

- Jury availability management.
- Team slot booking.
- Automatic blocking after slot selection.
- Region-based access controls.
- External video meeting integration for prototype.

## FAQ Chatbot

- FAQ chatbot to answer common student queries.
- Admin-manageable FAQ knowledge base.
